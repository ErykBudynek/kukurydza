# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS (no framework), chosen for zero-friction deploy and full control over craft, motion, and typography. Confirmed by user.

## Users

Designers, frontend engineers, and Cursor users who want to see what the Impeccable skill can produce when given a playful, unexpected subject. They arrive curious, often comparing AI design output, and decide in seconds whether the craft feels out of distribution.

## Product Purpose

A single landing page about corn (kukurydza) that exists as a living demonstration of Impeccable. Success means a first-time visitor remembers a specific visual idea about corn, not a generic agri marketing site, and understands this page was built as a skill showcase.

## Positioning

Corn treated as a cultural and botanical subject with editorial ambition, not as a farm-goods catalog or food brand funnel. The page is the proof of the skill; corn is the vehicle.

## Operating Context

Opened locally or via a simple static host. Viewed on desktop and mobile. Consumed in one sitting: scroll, feel the craft, optionally share. Polish language is the primary voice of the surface.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One self-contained static site (HTML, CSS, JS)
- No backend, no CMS, no auth
- No real commercial claims, customers, prices, or farm partnerships
- Synthetic demonstration content is allowed when clearly labeled as illustrative
- Design must be maximized: distinctive world, authored motion, real imagery where it earns its keep

## Brand Commitments

- Subject: kukurydza / corn
- Role: przedstawienie skilla Impeccable
- Voice: Polish, confident, sensory, never corporate agri-speak
- User asked for design maxed out (wymaksowana pod kątem designu)

## Evidence on Hand

No prior brand assets, photography library, or customer proof. Future work must not invent farm clients, yields, or certifications. Imagery and illustrative stats may be authored or sourced for demonstration and labeled when they could be mistaken for product claims.

## Product Principles

1. The page proves craft by how corn is seen, not by claiming the skill is good.
2. One committed visual world beats a tour of effects.
3. Sensory specificity over generic abundance metaphors.
4. Polish voice with editorial precision; no hype filler.
5. Static and fast enough that the design does the talking.
